Mission brief
DOC 01 / WHY GDG

  

    

      
I’m a first-year mechatronics student, so my days are split between motors and microcontrollers and the web apps I build at night. In a few months I shipped four of them: a wedding planner, a study planner, a notes-to-flashcards tool, and a badminton coach that runs pose detection in the browser.

      
Every one taught me the same lesson. The best part of building is when a stranger uses it without me explaining anything. Google’s tools make that moment possible at scale: Firebase and Google Cloud for backends, the Gemini API for smart features, Android and PWAs for every phone.

      
I want to learn how these tools are meant to be used from the engineers who ship them. If I’m selected, I’ll turn it into workshops for first-years on my campus who have ideas but don’t know where to start.
